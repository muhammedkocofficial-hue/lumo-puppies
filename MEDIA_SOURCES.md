## Bu düzenlemede üretilen editoryal görseller
Yöntem: yerleşik image_gen aracı. Görseller dekoratif/editoryal kullanımdadır; müşteri, sahiplendirilmiş yavru veya sağlık belgesi kaydı olarak kullanılmaz.

### public/media/editorial/pomeranian.webp
Prompt: Generate a single photorealistic editorial photograph for a refined Turkish puppy brand website, horizontal 3:2. A small healthy orange sable Pomeranian with natural fox face and fluffy double coat standing on pale travertine courtyard tiles beside olive foliage, soft morning sunlight, candid dignified stance, full body in frame with ample breathing room, realistic anatomy, tactile fur, ivory and muted olive palette. No text, no logos, no collars with words, no awards. This is an illustrative breed portrait, not documentary evidence. Save as an asset for web.

### public/media/editorial/story.webp
Prompt: Horizontal 3:2 photorealistic editorial lifestyle photograph. Close view of a person's relaxed hands gently greeting a small apricot toy poodle at ground level in a sunlit elegant cream living room. The dog approaches voluntarily with relaxed posture. Natural realistic anatomy, soft film texture, linen and light oak, heartwarming but not staged, premium magazine composition, low angle, faces of humans not visible, no text, no logos. For a puppy brand's story section, illustrative photography, not a customer testimonial.

### public/media/editorial/care.webp
Prompt: Horizontal 3:2 photorealistic still life editorial photograph for a dog care article. On a pale oak table a wooden slicker brush, stainless steel rounded comb, folded ivory cotton towel, a plain ceramic water bowl, warm window light and muted sage backdrop, refined tactile real materials, minimalist but lived in. No text, no logos, no dog, no medical tools. A practical grooming kit for toy poodle care.

### public/media/editorial/home.webp
Prompt: Horizontal 3:2 photorealistic premium interior editorial photograph for preparing a home for a puppy: small soft oatmeal dog bed on floor, low ceramic water bowl nearby, two tasteful safe rubber dog toys, puppy gate in doorway softly out of focus. Sunlit warm modest apartment with linen curtains, uncluttered floor, cream and walnut palette. No people, no animals, no text, no logos. Natural shadows realistic textures, not overly perfect showroom.

Önceki `public/media/examples/` editoryal görsellerinin kökeni değişmedi. Kullanıcı talebiyle ön yüzdeki üretim etiketleri kaldırıldı. Gerçek olmayan yavru envanteri ve müşteri hikâyeleri yayına taşınmadı.
